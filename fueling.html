How I Fuel for My Races

What I eat and drink before, during, and after a race.

Home Fueling Shoes Training

Before the Race

I eat more carbs like pasta and rice for two days before the race. On race morning I have a plain coffee, sip on electrolytes, and have sour dough banana honey toast, about two hours before the start.

During the Race

I take an energy gel with water every 30 to 45 minutes or every 4 miles. NEVER try anything new on race day.

After the Race

  • Drink water and eat something salty.
  • Eat carbs and protein within an hour.
  • Eat a full meal later.
