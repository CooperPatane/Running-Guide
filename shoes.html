Daily Trainer

This is the Adidas Evo SL, I wear this for most of my easy runs and long runs. It is comfortable and built to handle a lot of miles.

Race-Day Shoe

This is the Nike Alpha fly, I use this for races and hard workouts. I wear it on a few runs first so it is broken in before race day.

When to Replace Shoes

Running shoes usually last about 300 to 500 miles. I replace mine when the cushioning feels flat or my legs start to ache.
