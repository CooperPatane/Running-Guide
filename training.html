My Weekly Schedule

Most of my runs are easy. I follow a plan that progressively increases my weekly milage and speed.

Building Up

  1. Build easy mileage first.
  2. Add longer long runs and harder workouts.
  3. Cut back the mileage for the last two to three weeks before the race.

Rest

I take rest days and make sleep a big priority, because that is when my body rebuilds and gets stronger. Go back to the home page to see the other topics.
